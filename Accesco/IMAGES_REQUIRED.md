## Image Specifications

### Service Icons (Items 1-5)
- **Recommended Size**: 400x400px or larger (square format)
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background OR with colored background
- **Style**: Should match the color scheme:
  - Grokly: Green (#c1ff72)
  - Swadishtt: Maroon (#8B1E3F)
  - InstaStyle: Purple/Blue (#7c8ff7)
  - DineOut: Pink (#ff94ae)
  - AccesGO: Blue (#5bb8ff)

### Logo
- **accesco_white.png**: White version of your logo, 120x120px minimum, transparent background
- **accesco_original.png**: Original colored logo for Partner page, 65x65px minimum

### Video
- **start.mp4**: Background video for hero section (optional)
- **poster.jpg**: Video poster/thumbnail image

### About Image
- **about.jpeg**: Company/team photo, recommended 600x400px minimum

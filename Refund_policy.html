Refund & Cancellation Policy Overview

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to all orders placed on the Accesco Living platform across all verticals — Grokly (grocery), Swadisht (food / cloud kitchen), DineX (dining), InstaStyle (fashion), and LocalMeds (pharmacy). This policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and fully compliant with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

1. Order Cancellation — By Customer

1.1 Cancellation Window

To keep ecosystem dynamics optimal, cancellation conditions vary dynamically across vertical delivery channels:

GroklyWithin 60s before store acceptance
SwadishtWithin 60s before preparation begins
DineXUp to 2 hours before reservation
InstaStyleWithin 10 minutes before dispatch
LocalMedsWithin 60s before Rx processing
SubscriptionsUp to 24h before next billing cycle

1.2 How to Cancel

  • Open Accesco Living App → My Orders → Select Order → Cancel Order.
  • Cancellation requests within the allowed window are processed instantly.
  • Confirmation will be sent via SMS and Push Notification.
  • DineX reservations may also be cancelled via email confirmation links.

1.3 Late Cancellation

Orders cancelled after the permitted window are generally not eligible for cancellation refunds unless covered under specific Refund Eligibility provisions.

2. Order Cancellation — By Accesco Living

2.1 When We May Cancel Your Order

  • Item unavailability at inventory touchpoints.
  • Unforeseeable rider delivery constraints.
  • Unreachable or unserviceable delivery locations.
  • Payment gateway verification failures.
  • Force majeure events or regulatory restrictions.
  • Systemic fraud detection algorithms triggered.

2.2 Notification

Customers will receive immediate notifications through SMS, Email, and Push Notifications. Full refunds are processed automatically into original modes.

3. Refund Eligibility

3.1 Full Refund Eligibility

  • Order cancelled within permitted cancellation window.
  • Order cancelled by Accesco Living.
  • Order completely failed or not delivered.
  • Incorrect or completely different item delivered.
  • Damaged, spilled, or spoiled products.
  • Quantity delivered is less than the billed quantity.
  • Food hygiene or verifiable quality issues.
  • Expired or incorrect pharmacy items.

3.2 Partial Refund Eligibility

  • Partial order line-item missing issues.
  • Significant service execution delays.
  • Manufacturing defects discovered in eligible fashion items.

3.3 Refund Amount

Refund calculations precisely include the affected product core value, applied dynamic taxes, and proportional delivery charges where validly relevant.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

4.1 Refund Processing

  • Refunds are systematically initiated within 24 hours post-internal approval validation.
  • Original gateway payment methods are utilized by secure engineering default.
  • Accesco Living Wallet refunds are available instantly upon selection.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be entertained under the following clear circumstances:

  • Change of mind after the allotted cancellation window closes.
  • Incorrect or un-contactable address parameters provided by customer.
  • Customer availability collapse during actual physical delivery window.
  • Partially consumed/used products or opened hygiene seals.
  • Promotional loyalty credits, campaign tokens, and vouchers.
  • Products explicitly marked as non-returnable in app fields.
  • Opened prescription medicines or activated digital micro-services.

6. Damaged, Incorrect & Missing Items

6.1 Reporting Window

Incidents must be registered strictly within the structured legal windows below:

GroklyWithin 6 Hours
SwadishtWithin 2 Hours
InstaStyleWithin 48 Hours
LocalMedsWithin 24 Hours
DineXDuring Experience Window

6.2 Evidence Required

  • Clear unedited cryptographic photo or video proof of anomalies.
  • Original barcode sticker and outer shipping packaging.

6.3 Resolution

Resolutions include instant standard replacement, clean system source refund, or structured wallet credit allocations with additional goodwill considerations.

7. Returns — InstaStyle

7.1 Return Eligibility

  • Return execution triggers inside 7 absolute calendar days from delivery.
  • Unused, completely unwashed, and mint-conditioned returns only.
  • Original price tags, accessory loops, and branded boxing must remain intact.

7.2 Non-Returnable Items

Innerwear, intimate wear, shapewear, cosmetics, final clearance sales, and personalized custom apparel fabrications.

7.3 Trial at Your Doorstep

Customers are securely charged strictly for verified elements they choose to officially keep post-doorstep physical trials.

7.4 Return Pickup

Physical collection operations map within 24–48 hours post-approval. Financial refunds cycle strictly following backward facility automated quality controls inspection.

8. Returns — LocalMeds

8.1 General Rule

Due to therapeutic clinical safety parameters and rigorous pharmaceutical tracking codes, medicines cannot arbitrarily be returned post-delivery.

8.2 Exception Configurations

  • Verifiably expired pharmaceutical goods.
  • Mismatched drug supply configurations vs prescription.
  • Compromised primary structural packaging or seal damage.
  • Systemic prescription verification workflow cancellation.

9. Subscription & Membership Cancellation

  • Users can toggle off recurring cycles via: Settings → Subscription.
  • Premium benefit paths remain fully active until the active billing cycle naturally expires.
  • No partial pro-rata refunds are calculated except within an exclusive 24-hour purchase window.
  • Automated warning notification updates route 3 days prior to payment execution.

10. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

  1. Raise immediate resolution tickets inside the secure In-App Support interface.
  2. Escalate unresolved tracking items directly to: support@accescoliving.in
  3. Grievance Officer point contact: grievance@accescoliving.in
  4. Unresolved corporate disputes follow statutory routes under the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission frameworks.

Unwarranted chargebacks or fraudulent claims may result in immediate permanent suspension of ecosystem account access privileges.

11. Governing Law

These policies are configured, processed, and governed in strict adherence with the laws of the Republic of India. Judicial courts operating in Bengaluru, Karnataka maintain exclusive operational jurisdiction.

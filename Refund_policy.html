Cancellation & Refund Policy

At ACCESCO Living, we strive to provide a transparent and customer-friendly experience. This policy outlines the terms governing cancellations, refunds, and related requests.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

```

This Refund and Cancellation Policy applies to all orders placed on the Accesco Living platform across all verticals — Grokly (grocery), Swadisht (food / cloud kitchen), DineX (dining), InstaStyle (fashion), and LocalMeds (pharmacy). This policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and compliant with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

```

1. Order Cancellation — By Customer

```

1.1 Cancellation Window

  • Grokly: Within 60 seconds before dark store acceptance.
  • Swadisht: Within 60 seconds before preparation begins.
  • DineX: Up to 2 hours before reservation.
  • InstaStyle: Within 10 minutes before dispatch.
  • LocalMeds: Within 60 seconds before prescription processing.
  • Subscriptions: Up to 24 hours before next billing cycle.

1.2 How to Cancel

  • Open Accesco Living App → My Orders → Select Order → Cancel Order.
  • Cancellation requests within the allowed window are processed instantly.
  • Confirmation will be sent via SMS and Push Notification.
  • DineX reservations may also be cancelled via email confirmation links.

1.3 Late Cancellation

Orders cancelled after the permitted window are generally not eligible for cancellation refunds unless covered under Refund Eligibility provisions.

```

2. Order Cancellation — By Accesco Living

```

2.1 When We May Cancel Your Order

  • Item unavailability.
  • No rider availability.
  • Unreachable delivery location.
  • Payment verification failure.
  • Force majeure events.
  • Fraud detection.
  • Regulatory restrictions.

2.2 Notification

Customers will receive notification through SMS, Email, and Push Notifications. Full refunds are processed automatically.

```

3. Refund Eligibility

```

3.1 Full Refund Eligibility

  • Order cancelled within permitted cancellation window.
  • Order cancelled by Accesco Living.
  • Order not delivered.
  • Incorrect item delivered.
  • Damaged or spoiled products.
  • Product significantly different from listing.
  • Quantity delivered less than billed quantity.
  • Food quality issues.
  • Expired or incorrect pharmacy items.

3.2 Partial Refund Eligibility

  • Partial order issues.
  • Significant service delays.
  • Manufacturing defects in eligible fashion items.

3.3 Refund Amount

Refunds include the affected product value, taxes, and applicable delivery charges where relevant.

```

4. Refund Process & Timelines

```

4.1 Refund Processing

  • Refunds are initiated within 24 hours after approval.
  • Original payment method is used by default.
  • Accesco Living Wallet refunds are available instantly upon request.

Refund Timeline

  • UPI: Instant to 2 Business Days
  • Credit Card: 5–7 Business Days
  • Debit Card: 5–7 Business Days
  • Net Banking: 3–5 Business Days
  • Wallet: Instant
  • COD: Wallet or Bank Transfer within 5–7 Business Days
  • BNPL / EMI: 5–7 Business Days

4.2 Refund Tracking

Refund status can be tracked through My Orders → Refund Status.

```

5. Non-Refundable Situations

```
  • Change of mind after cancellation window.
  • Incorrect address provided by customer.
  • Customer unavailable during delivery.
  • Partially consumed products.
  • Promotional credits and vouchers.
  • Products marked as non-returnable.
  • Opened prescription medicines.
  • Activated digital services.
```

6. Damaged, Incorrect & Missing Items

```

6.1 Reporting Window

  • Grokly: Within 6 Hours
  • Swadisht: Within 2 Hours
  • InstaStyle: Within 48 Hours
  • LocalMeds: Within 24 Hours
  • DineX: During Experience

6.2 Evidence Required

  • Photo or video proof.
  • Original packaging.
  • Order ID verification.

6.3 Resolution

Resolution may include replacement, refund, or wallet credit with additional goodwill compensation.

```

7. Returns — InstaStyle

```

7.1 Return Eligibility

  • Return request within 7 days.
  • Unused, unwashed condition.
  • Original tags and packaging intact.
  • Not listed under non-returnable products.

7.2 Non-Returnable Items

  • Innerwear and lingerie.
  • Final sale products.
  • Customized products.
  • Customer-damaged products.

7.3 Trial at Your Doorstep

Customers are charged only for items they keep after trial.

7.4 Return Pickup

Pickup occurs within 24–48 hours after approval. Refunds begin after quality inspection.

```

8. Returns — LocalMeds

```

8.1 General Rule

Most medicines cannot be returned after delivery due to regulatory requirements.

8.2 Refund Eligible Cases

  • Expired medicines.
  • Wrong medication supplied.
  • Damaged packaging.
  • Short quantity supplied.
  • Prescription verification cancellation.

8.3 Non-Returnable

  • Opened medicines.
  • Cold-chain medicines.
  • Controlled substances.
```

9. Subscription & Membership Cancellation

```
  • Cancel anytime via Settings → Subscription.
  • Benefits remain active until billing cycle ends.
  • No pro-rata refunds except within 24 hours of purchase.
  • Auto-renewal reminders are sent 3 days before renewal.
```

10. Dispute Resolution

```
  1. Raise issue via In-App Support.
  2. Email: support@accescoliving.in
  3. Escalate to grievance@accescoliving.in
  4. Approach Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.

Unwarranted chargebacks may result in suspension of platform access.

```

11. Governing Law

```

These policies are governed by the laws of the Republic of India. Courts in Bengaluru, Karnataka shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
```

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all orders placed on the Accesco Living platform across all verticals — Grokly (grocery), Swadisht, DineX, InstaStyle, LocalMeds, FarmChain, and Xpense Meter.

This policy outlines conditions under which cancellations are permitted, timeline parameters, and execution workflows for refunds processed through domestic banking networks.

1. Company Information & Scope

1.1 Who We Are

Accesco Living Private Limited is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in West Bengal and primary operations based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

1.2 Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to all users of the Accesco Living platform, including registered customers, guest users, delivery partners, dark store staff, and any individual whose personal data is processed by us.

1.3 Consent

By creating an account, placing an order, or otherwise using our services, you expressly consent to the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of your personal information as described in this policy.

  • Delivery addresses.
  • Payment information.
  • Identity verification documents.
  • Health and dietary preferences.
  • Support communications and feedback.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information.
  • Location data.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • To process and fulfil orders.

  • To detect fraud and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

  • Delivery partners.

8. Children's Privacy
